September 14-16, 2026 [posit::conf(2026)](https://conf.posit.co/2026/) will take place in Houston, Texas. 

* [Emily Riederer](/author/emily-riederer), editor for software review, will give a [keynote talk](https://conf.posit.co/2026/sessions/) on Wednesday, September 16, at 9:00AM CDT. 

* [Luis D. Verde Arregoitia](/author/luis-d.-verde-arregoitia/), mentor in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 champions cohorts, will present ["Can I offer you a nice IDE in this trying time?"](https://conf.posit.co/20riderer26/sessions/) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, at 10:40 PM CDT.

* [Jon Harmon](/author/jon-harmon/), former gutenbergr maintainer, will present ["The Design of Everyday R Functions"](https://conf.posit.co/2026/sessions/) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, at 3:40-4pm CDT.
