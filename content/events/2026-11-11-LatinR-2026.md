📍 Medellín, Colombia · November 11–13, 2026 · All times local (UTC-5)
🔗 Full program: https://latinr.org/cronograma/programa/detallado.html


### Wednesday, November 11

**Tutorial: LLMs in R using {ellmer}** (English)
8:30–12:00 · Auditorio principal
Nic Crane (rOpenSci community member)
How to use LLMs programmatically in R with ellmer: connecting to LLM APIs, extracting structured data from unstructured text, and letting LLMs call R functions and access external sources for more reliable results.
[More info](https://latinr.org/cronograma/tutoriales/index.html#taller-ellmer)

**Tutorial: De idea a paquete: programá tu primer paquete de R** (Spanish)
14:00–17:30 · Auditorio 2
Yanina Bellini Saibene (rOpenSci Community Manager)
A hands-on workshop to turn an idea into an R package: project organization, coding, documentation, testing and publishing, including the use of AI (GitHub Copilot) as a development assistant.
[More info](https://latinr.org/cronograma/tutoriales/index.html#taller-paquete-r)

### Thursday, November 12

**Keynote: Learning with AI: Code, Data, and Life**
8:30–9:30
Nic Crane (rOpenSci community member)

*Session "Paquetes, frameworks e infraestructura", 10:00–12:00 · Auditorio 1*

**Continuing our journey through the R universe**
Jeroen Ooms (rOpenSci Lead Infrastructure Engineer)
A second mission through R-universe: how to navigate the ecosystem, use its APIs and tools, and make your packages more visible and easier to find.

**Currículo del Programa de Campeon(a|e)s de rOpenSci para el desarrollo y mantenimiento de paquetes de R**
Yanina Bellini Saibene (rOpenSci staff), Maëlle Salmon (rOpenSci staff), Paola Corrales (rOpenSci Champion 2022–2023 and Mentor), Alejandra Bellini
The open curriculum of the rOpenSci Champions Program, in Spanish and English: its pedagogical approach, materials, and the results of independent evaluations.

**Explorando el lupus en México: datos abiertos y análisis reproducibles en R**
Evelia Lorena Coss Navarrete (rOpenSci Champion 2026–2027)
A data package for learning R and bioinformatics, built from transcriptomic data of Mexican women with lupus and healthy controls.

### Friday, November 13

**HackeaR: contribution hackathon to base R and rOpenSci projects**
8:00–10:00 · Auditorio principal
Two hours of work in small groups to make real contributions to base R and rOpenSci projects: analyzing R bugs, proposing fixes, working on issues and reviewing translations of rOpenSci materials. Newcomers welcome!
Mentors from the rOpenSci community:
- Yanina Bellini Saibene (staff, co-organizer)
- Jeroen Ooms (staff)
- Beatriz Milz (Software Peer Review Editor and Champions Mentor)
- Paola Corrales (Champion and Mentor)
- Elio Campitelli (Champions Mentor)
- Luis D. Verde Arregoitia (Champions Mentor)
- Francisco Cardozo (Champions Mentor)
- Diana Garcia (Champion 2025–2026)

Together with Riva Quiroga, Heather Turner and Ella Kaye.
[More info](https://latinr.org/cronograma/hackear/) · [rOpenSci Help Wanted issues](https://ropensci.org/help-wanted/) · [R Devel HackeaR dashboard](https://github.com/orgs/r-devel/projects/14/)

**El valor de aprender y enseñar R en la era de la IA**
10:30–12:10 (session "Inteligencia artificial") · Auditorio 1
Luis D. Verde Arregoitia (rOpenSci Champions Mentor) and Jesica Formoso
How generative AI tools affect R teaching, and a proposal to adapt education by focusing on understanding and modifying AI-generated code and on the value of communities.
